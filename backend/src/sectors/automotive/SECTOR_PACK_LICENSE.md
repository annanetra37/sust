# Automotive Sector Pack — Licence

## Content

- **KPI definitions**: derived from SASB TR-AU, ESRS, EU fleet CO2 regulation, Catena-X, and EU Battery Regulation public documents.
- **Materiality starters**: in-house, referencing BMW 2024 Sustainability Report, VW 2024 Sustainability Report, and EU regulatory texts.
- **Emission factors**: sourced from Ecoinvent 3.10 (public subset), DEFRA 2024, and the GREET Model (Argonne National Laboratory, public).
- **Drivetrain use-phase factors**: derived from published WLTP homologation data and DEFRA 2024 conversion factors.

## Attribution

Factor provenance is stored in `EmissionFactor.source` and carried through to `PcfCalculation.factorSnapshot`.
